Kopor

The business book, payments and advisor for every small business. Works offline, speaks Krio, and turns daily records into access to credit.

"Kopor" means money in Krio.

02 The problem

Small businesses run on a paper notebook. It forgets, it gets lost, and it cannot get them a loan.

Aminata · 2 bag rice1,700
Mohamed · oil360
Isatu · ??? (paid half?)?
Abu · sugar, maggi96
Profit this weekunknown
  • Debts are forgotten or disputed. Credit ("trust") is everyday business, and the notebook is the only record.
  • Owners don't know their profit. Cash, Orange Money and Afrimoney mix with household money.
  • No records means no credit. Only 17% of Sierra Leone's SMEs have a formal loan or credit line.
03 Who we serve

Small businesses are Sierra Leone's economy.

98.5%of businesses are micro, small or medium
84%of employment comes from MSMEs
~70%of GDP is produced by MSMEs
17%of SMEs can get formal credit

Shop owners, market traders, wholesalers, pharmacies, salons and tailors, plus the osusu groups, associations, churches and schools that collect money every week. Most are informal, many are women-led, and nearly all already use a phone and mobile money.

08 Go-to-market

Market by market, group by group.

Market agents

Local agents sign up shops in Freetown's markets (Kissy Road, Dove Cot, Big Market) and earn a commission for every shop still active after 30 days.

Osusu and associations

One group leader brings 10 to 50 members. Every reminder and group update spreads Kopor through WhatsApp.

Lender partners

Microfinance institutions recommend Kopor to borrowers because it gives them clean repayment and sales data.

Phase 1: Freetown pilot with 50 shops and 5 osusu groups. Phase 2: Bo, Kenema, Makeni. Phase 3: Liberia and Guinea (English and French are already built in).
