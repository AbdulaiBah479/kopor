## At a market association meeting (2 minutes)

1. Ask: "Who here has lost money because a customer never paid, or cash went missing?" (hands go up)
2. Show one phone: record a sale by speaking in Krio, then Home shows today's profit.
3. Show the credit book and a WhatsApp reminder.
4. Offer: "Free to try. I will set it up on your phone today."
5. Write down names and numbers; follow up within 2 days.

- What confused you? Where did you get stuck?
