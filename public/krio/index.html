KKopor In English

Yu biznɛs buk, insay yu pɔkit

Kopor na fri app fɔ shɔp, trayda ɛn smɔl biznɛs na Salone. Kip yu trɔs buk, kɛsh buk, stɔk ɛn mobile money na wan ples, na Krio ɔ na Inglish, ivin if no intanɛt.

Wetin Kopor de du

  • Trɔs bukUdat de ɔl yu mɔni, ɔmɔs, ɛn i de mɛmba dɛn wit SMS ɔ WhatsApp.
  • Kɛsh buk ɛn prɔfitWetin yu sɛl ɛn wetin yu spɛn, ɛn yu prɔfit fɔ tide.
  • StɔkNo wetin de dɔn, ɛn sɛn ɔda to yu sɔplaya.
  • Mobile moneyGɛt pe wit Orange Money ɔ Africell money. Kopor de rayt am ɛn tɛl yu if dɛn rivas am.
  • Osusu ɛn sev mɔniRɔn osusu grup wit klia rɛkɔd fɔ ɔlman.
  • Invoice ɛn risitSɛn invoice ɛn risit, ɛn mek pipul pe smɔl smɔl.
  • Dola rɛtDi dola rɛt na Lion fɔ tide, wi de chɛk am ɛvri awa.
  • Tɔk na KrioTɔk na Krio ɛn Kopor go rayt am fɔ yu.
