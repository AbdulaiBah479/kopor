KKopor Na Krio

Your business book, in your pocket

Kopor is a free app for shops, traders and small businesses in Sierra Leone. Keep your credit book, cash book, stock and mobile money payments in one place, in English or Krio, even with no internet.

Open Kopor, it's free

What Kopor does

  • Credit book (trust book)Who owes you, how much, and friendly reminders by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Cash book and profitSales and expenses, with today's profit and a closing summary every evening.
  • StockKnow what is running low and send a restock order to your supplier.
  • Mobile moneyGet paid by Orange Money or Africell money. Kopor records it and warns you about reversals.
  • Osusu and savingsRun osusu groups and savings jars with clear records for every member.
  • Invoices and receiptsSend invoices, receipts and quotations, with instalments and layaway.
  • Dollar rateToday's US dollar rate in Leones, checked every hour.
  • Krio assistantSpeak in Krio and Kopor writes it down for you.
  • Staff and branchesGive cashiers their own login and see every branch from your phone.

Questions

Is Kopor free?

Yes. Kopor is free for one shop with up to 3 staff. Kopor Pro costs Le 75 a month and adds more branches, more staff and more AI help.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is saved on your phone first and backs up to the cloud when you are online.

Does it work with Orange Money and Africell money?

Yes. Customers can pay you through Monime, and Kopor records the payment and alerts you about reversals.

Can I use it in Krio?

Yes. Kopor speaks English, Krio and French.
